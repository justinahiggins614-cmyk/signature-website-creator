💰 Selling & payments, in plain words

We stay out legally. If you add selling to your site, payments are between you and your own payment provider (like your own Stripe account). The Signature Website Creator never holds, routes, or processes money, and never stores card numbers or secrets. Checkout on created sites is opt-in and off by default — you connect your own provider and flip it on yourself, in your site's Manager settings.

❓ Honest answers

Is the "AI" real?
The builder is a deterministic assembler: it reads your choices and snaps real components together, then tells you exactly what it picked and why. The "AI Llama" options wire in the genuine free Signature Llama engine — online loads it live, offline looks for it in a local folder.

Where do my sites live?
In your browser's local storage (My Websites tab) and in the files you download. We keep no copy and no account data.

What does the manager password do?
It unlocks the 🛗 Manager panel on your downloaded site so you can edit text, set prices, and connect your own payment links. Only the password's fingerprint is baked into your files.
