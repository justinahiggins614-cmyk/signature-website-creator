Welcome to Signature Website Creator

Describe the website you want — the AI builds it for real. Then edit, lock in, and make it yours. Here’s how:

  1. Describe it. Tell the builder what site you want — pick static, Llama-online, or Llama-offline.
  2. Edit live. The preview shows your site with edit controls at the bottom — change anything, then press Done.
  3. Lock it in. When it looks complete, the Lock In button highlights — lock it, set your name and a manager password.
  4. Make it yours. After lock-in: set prices with your own provider, or mirror any site’s layout with your own brand.

How to use this site

🧬 AI builder

What: the builder page. Does: Describe a website in plain words and the AI builds a real working site. How: Open the builder, describe, pick static / Llama-online / Llama-offline.

✏️ Live editing

What: the edit controls. Does: Your site previewed live with edit controls pinned at the bottom — press Done when finished. How: Edit in the preview; Done hands you the highlighted Lock In button.

🔒 Lock In

What: the commit step. Does: Locks your site in — you set your name and a manager/editor master password for full manual control after. How: Press the highlighted Lock In when the site looks complete.

🎨 1 Million Website Options

What: the options catalog. Does: Templates and designs marching to a million, on their own tab. How: The Options tab — browse and apply.

🪾 Mirror a Website

What: the mirror page. Does: Paste any URL — the tool rebuilds the layout structure with YOUR images, products, and brand. Layout inspiration only — never the source’s trademarks. How: The Mirror tab; paste a URL and apply your brand.

🌐 My Websites

What: your sites. Does: Every site you’ve built, with edit and delete (in-page confirmations, never popups). How: The My Websites tab.

💳 Your money, your provider

What: pricing and checkout. Does: After lock-in you set prices and retail info on your own site with your own provider — card checkout is built in but strictly opt-in. How: Follow the post-lock-in prompts; the builder never touches your money.
